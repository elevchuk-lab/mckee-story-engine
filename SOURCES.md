# Primary Sources & Bibliographical Reference Registry

This document catalogs the verified primary sources, publishers, ISBN-13 identifiers, and documented citations for all five works in Robert McKee's pentalogy formalized within the **Robert McKee Story Engine**.

It serves as the factual baseline for contributors and automated regression tests to prevent unverified additions or erroneous modifications.

## 2. Theoretical Scope & Attribution Boundaries

1. **Core Pentalogy vs. Pedagogical Extensions:**
   All modern breakdowns of recent media (*Dark Matter*, *Succession*, *Breaking Bad*, video game mission rubrics) are pedagogical extensions created by the repository author to illustrate McKee's theory in modern contexts. They are explicitly demarcated as modern extensions and must never be attributed to original books published prior to those works.
2. **Bibliographical Verification Standard:**
   Any addition of ISBNs, co-authors, or citation details must be verifiable via the official publisher catalog or national library registries. Speculative chapter numbers or unverified citations must not be added.
