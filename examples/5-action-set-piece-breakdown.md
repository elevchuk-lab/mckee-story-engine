# Example 5: Action & Suspense Pacing Doctor (Mode 5)

> Demonstrating how the McKee Story Engine transforms a generic, flat, AI-generated action sequence into a high-octane, suspense-engineered **Set Piece** compliant with Robert McKee & Bassim El-Wakil's ***Action*** (2022).

---

## 1. The Input (Generic AI Action Draft)

```text
Captain Vance ran down the metallic corridor of the orbital station as sirens blared. Laser blasts whizzed past his ears from the enemy security droids. Vance spun around, calmly fired three shots from his plasma rifle, and destroyed all three droids in rapid succession.

He checked his wrist computer. He had five minutes before the reactor exploded. He kicked open the blast door and stepped onto the gantry above the core. Standing there was General Kroll, clad in heavy battle armor.

"Give up, Vance," Kroll said with an evil laugh. "The station will self-destruct and humanity will fall!"

"Not on my watch," Vance replied with a grin. 

Kroll charged with his energy blade. Vance easily dodged the swing, rolled behind Kroll, and kicked him into the coolant vat. Kroll screamed as he fell. Vance grabbed the encryption keycard from the console, sprinted to the escape pod just in the nick of time, and launched into space as the station erupted in a massive fireball.
```

## 2. The McKee Diagnostic X-Ray (Mode 5)

```text
[STRUCTURAL AUDIT: ACTION GENRE FLACCIDITY]

1. CORE VALUE POLARITY:
   - Intended: Survival (+) vs. Death (-)
   - Reality: Flat (+ to +). Vance never faces genuine biological peril. The "5-minute countdown" has no mechanical impact on his actions.

2. POWER ASYMMETRY:
   - Fatal Defect: ZERO ASYMMETRY. Vance effortlessly headshots 3 droids in running stride, easily dodges Kroll's blade, and kicks the armored boss into a vat with a grin. Kroll is an incompetent clown; Vance has total plot armor.

3. THE ACTION TRIAD:
   - Hero: Vance displays zero altruistic sacrifice; he is a frictionless action figure.
   - Villain: Kroll is a cartoon villain with mustache-twirling monologue ("evil laugh").
   - Victim: Abstract "humanity"—no vulnerable human face in immediate jeopardy to anchor audience empathy.

4. THE GAP & SET PIECE ANATOMY:
   - The Gap: MISSING. Vance's subjective expectation ("I will kick the door and kill Kroll") matches objective reality 100%. The world never strikes back with an unforeseen obstacle.
   - Hero at the Mercy of the Villain: MISSING. Vance is in complete control at every micro-second.
   - Cost of Survival: ZERO. Vance leaves without a scratch, retaining all resources.
```

## 3. The McKee Engine Rewrite (Set Piece Engineering)

```text
[STRUCTURAL X-RAY]
- Mode: ACTION & PACING DOCTOR
- Core Value & Shift: Survival vs. Annihilation (+ fragile breach -> - lethal entrapment -> -- cornered at mercy -> + agonizing survival at permanent bodily cost)
- Controlling Idea: Survival (+) is secured only when the altruistic hero trades personal physical integrity for the life of the vulnerable.
- The Action Triad:
  * Hero: Vance (Severely concussed, 4 rounds remaining, outclassed by military chassis).
  * Villain: General Kroll (Genetically augmented exo-suit, hydraulic crushing strength, tactical patience).
  * Victim: Specialist Maya Lin (Trapped inside the pressurized diagnostic pod, oxygen reserves draining at 12% per minute).
- The MacGuffin: The Cryo-Inhibitor Rod (without it, the reactor vents neutron radiation across the civilian transit corridor).
- Power Asymmetry: Kroll outweighs Vance by 200 pounds of powered titanium. Vance cannot pierce Kroll's armor with small arms fire.

---

### SCENE: THE CATWALK OVER SECTOR FOUR

The pressure bulkhead didn't open. The hydraulic seal had buckled under heat, leaking a white hiss of superheated freon across the floor grates. 

Vance slammed his shoulder against the manual release lever. Metal sheared through the fabric of his flight suit, chewing into his collarbone. The door cracked open three inches. Inside the pressure chamber, Maya’s face was pressed against the reinforced acrylic. Her helmet gauge blinked amber: `08% O2`. 

A deafening metallic thud vibrated through the steel deck.

Vance spun, dropping his center of gravity. 

General Kroll stepped through the exhaust fog at the far end of the gantry. Seven feet of carbon-weave armor. No grandstanding. No evil grin. In Kroll's left gauntlet hung the magnetic lock of the cryo-inhibitor rod—the single valve pin that could shut down the core before thermal runaway.

Vance raised the sidearm with both hands. Four rounds left in the ceramic chamber. 

*Crack. Crack.*

Two high-velocity slugs flattened against Kroll’s thoracic plate like lead pennies, sparking harmlessly into the dark. Kroll didn't flinch. He didn't even accelerate his pace. He adjusted his grip on the hydraulic combat wrench in his right hand.

"Vance," Kroll said, voice flat, filtered through the suit’s barometric intake. "You have sixty-four seconds before the seals boil. Put the sidearm down and I will let the girl die in her sleep."

*The Gap opens:* Vance anticipated drawing Kroll into a crossfire on the stairs; instead, Kroll anchored himself directly on the narrow single-file gantry, cutting off all flank routes. 

Vance glanced back at the acrylic viewport. Maya was tapping three fingers against the glass in a rhythmic stutter: *SOS*. Her lips were already turning the bruised purple of hypoxia.

Vance fired his third round not at Kroll, but at the high-pressure hydraulic feed running along the ceiling above Kroll's head.

The line ruptured. Black mineral oil sprayed across Kroll’s optical sensors.

Kroll swung the wrench blindly in a horizontal arc. The kinetic shockwave clipped Vance’s left forearm—bone snapped with the wet sound of dry pine branches cracking under boots. Vance choked on a scream, dropped to one knee, the sidearm skittering five feet away into the drain grating.

*Hero at the Mercy of the Villain:*
Kroll wiped the visor clear with his forearm. He stepped onto Vance's shattered wrist, driving his full motorized weight down until Vance's forehead pressed against the burning steel deck.

"You're an infantryman, Vance," Kroll murmured, bringing the pointed pry-bar of the wrench toward Vance’s throat. "You believe willpower stops physics."

The coolant warning buzzer screamed three long blasts: *T-minus 30 seconds.*

Vance didn't reach for the gun. With his operational right hand, he unclipped the emergency magnesium road flare from his tactical harness. 

He didn't strike the flare against Kroll. He drove the ignition pin straight into the ruptured mineral oil pooling under Kroll's magnetized boots.

White thermite flame erupted instantly at four thousand degrees Fahrenheit.

The hydraulic oil flashed into a roaring chemical wall. The intense thermal bloom instantly short-circuited the exo-suit's localized servo motors, freezing Kroll's leg joints into locked position. Kroll roared—not in cartoon anger, but in primal human shock as the battery pack on his spine began cook-off expansion.

Vance didn't watch him burn. Dragging his useless left arm behind him, his vision tunneling through black shock, Vance lunged through the flames, wrenched the cryo-inhibitor rod from Kroll’s smoking gauntlet, and drove it home into the primary coolant manifold.

The reactor hum dropped from a scream to an exhausted moan.

Vance collapsed against the glass of the observation pod, forehead resting against the acrylic. Inside, Maya drew her first ragged breath as the emergency oxygen bypass kicked in. Through the blood in his eyes, Vance watched the escape pod eject into the black silence of orbit—leaving him alone on the burning catwalk with thirty seconds of air.
```

## 4. Cross-Medium Adaptation Breakdown

| Medium | How Mode 5 Adapts This Set Piece |
| :--- | :--- |
| **SCREEN (Film / TV)** | • Spatial geography: Clear line of sight between the manual lever (goal), Kroll (obstacle), and the oil pipe (environmental weapon).<br>• Audio counterpoint: Cuts from deafening mechanical alarms to the dead silence of Maya suffocating behind double-pane acrylic.<br>• Visual cost: The audience hears and sees the compound fracture of Vance's arm, eliminating all sense of superhero invulnerability. |
| **PAGE (Novel / Prose)** | • Visceral sensory vocabulary: *mineral oil, superheated freon, bone snapped like dry pine branches, thermite flame*.<br>• Syntactic pacing: Short, staccato clauses during the flare strike; longer, sensory-dense sentences during the suffocating dread of the "Hero at Mercy" pin.<br>• Internal stakes: The cold calculation of Vance realizing that to save Maya, he must permanently sacrifice his arm and his chance of evacuation. |
| **GAME (Interactive / Level Design)** | • Ludonarrative integration: The player's HUD flickers as arm functionality is disabled (one-handed controls only; aiming stability dropped by 75%).<br>• Environmental puzzle: The player must notice the oil line and remember the flare in their inventory rather than waiting for a Quick-Time Event (QTE).<br>• Emergent tension: The oxygen gauge of the companion pod creates a natural, diegetic ticking clock without artificial UI countdown timers. |
