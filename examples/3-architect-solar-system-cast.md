# Example 3: `[MODE: ARCHITECT]` — Solar System Cast & 5-Axis Contradiction Grid

> **User Prompt:**
> *"[ARCHITECT] I'm developing a prestige corporate/psychological thriller series about the founder of a longevity biotech company in Zurich who discovers his flagship gene therapy causes irreversible personality erasure in 3% of patients. Design the Protagonist using McKee's 5-Axis Contradiction Grid and build the supporting cast as a Solar System around him."*

---

## `[STRUCTURAL X-RAY]`
- **Mode:** `ARCHITECT` (Character Dimensionality & Solar System Cast Design)
- **Core Binary Value:** *Moral Mortality / Truth (`+`)* vs. *God-Complex / Manufactured Immortality (`--`)*
- **Controlling Idea:** *Human dignity (`+`) requires accepting mortality and moral limits, whereas playing God to conquer death (`Cause`) erases the very humanity it claims to preserve (`--`).*
- **The Crisis Dilemma (`+ vs +` / `- vs -`):** *In the Season 1 Climax, the protagonist's own terminally ill daughter is the only match for the unpatched trial batch: he must choose between letting her die intact as herself (`-`) or saving her biological life while knowing the therapy will erase her memory and personality (`--`).*

---

## 1. Protagonist Architecture: Dr. Julian Vance (The Sun)

### Outer Characterization (The Observable Mask)
- **Public & Personal Self:** 48-year-old former pediatric neurosurgeon turned biotech visionary in Zurich. Soft-spoken, ascetic, wears unadorned cashmere, listens intently, never raises his voice in boardrooms. Quotes Seneca on acceptance while selling a $400,000 cellular rejuvenation therapy.

### True Character (Revealed Only Under Dilemma)
- Beneath his monastic calm lives a **messianic tyrant** who cannot tolerate randomness, grief, or any outcome he did not personally author. When cornered, he will falsify data and sacrifice individuals to protect "the species-level breakthrough."

### The 5-Axis Contradiction Grid (McKee's Law of Dimensionality)

| Contradiction Axis | Surface Pole (Mask / Characterization) | Deep Pole (Shadow / True Character Under Fire) | Dramatic Detonation in Plot |
|--------------------|----------------------------------------|------------------------------------------------|-----------------------------|
| **1. Morality Axis** | Compassionate healer obsessed with ending human suffering | Utilitarian calculator who views individual patients as statistical noise | Quietly buries Patient #14's cognitive collapse as "pre-existing dementia" to clear Phase III FDA review. |
| **2. Power Axis** | Collaborative, egalitarian lab leader ("First among equals") | Ruthless autocrat who weaponizes his employees' deepest insecurities | Promotes his insecure lead geneticist right after discovering she noticed the anomaly—buying her silence with status. |
| **3. Truth / Intellect Axis** | Rigorous empiricist ("Data is sacred") | Magical thinker blinded by his own savior myth | Convincing himself he can patch a fatal epigenetic cascade *after* commercial rollout. |
| **4. Intimacy Axis** | Devoted, grieving widower who worships his late wife's memory | Emotionally relieved by her death because a memory cannot argue with him | Panics and sabotages his relationship with a living woman the moment she challenges his authority. |
| **5. Mortality Axis** | Preaches biological longevity and fearless aging | Secretly terrified of his own genetic Huntington's marker | Uses himself as an unlogged shadow subject at night. |

## 2. Cast Design as a Solar System

Per McKee's *Character* (2021), secondary characters do not exist for decoration; **each Planet is engineered to exert gravitational pull on a specific Contradiction Axis of the Protagonist (The Sun):**

```text
                       [Planet 2: Dr. Elena Rostova]
                        (Pressures Truth vs. Myth)
                                   │
                                   ▼
[Planet 1: Clara Vance] ───► ☀️ DR. JULIAN VANCE ◄─── [Planet 3: Marcus Vane]
(Pressures Healer vs.          (The Protagonist)       (Pressures Ascetic vs.
 Ruthless Utilitarian)                 ▲                Predatory Capital)
                                       │
                       [Planet 4: Patient #14 / Lukas]
                        (Living Mirror of the "--" State)
```

### Planet 1: Clara Vance (19, His Daughter) — *Gravitational Pull on Axis 1 (Healer vs. Utilitarian)*
- **Function:** Julian can easily rationalize sacrificing anonymous trial patients in Basel as "acceptable statistical variance" (`3%`). Clara carries the exact familial neurological degradation that prompted Julian's research—turning an abstract statistic into his own flesh and blood.
- **Her Contradiction:** Appears fragile and dependent on her father (*Mask*), but possesses a ferocious moral clarity that refuses to survive at the cost of someone else's cover-up (*True Character*).

### Planet 2: Dr. Elena Rostova (36, Chief Computational Biologist) — *Gravitational Pull on Axis 2 & 3 (Truth vs. Self-Deception)*
- **Function:** The brilliant immigrant scientist whose career Julian built. When she discovers the 3% personality-erasure cluster in the hippocampal methylation logs, Julian doesn't threaten her—he makes her Co-Founder (`Action: Bribing via Ambition`).
- **Her Contradiction:** Fiercely incorruptible whistleblower toward outsiders (*Mask*), yet desperate for a father figure's approval (*Shadow*), making her complicity agonizing step by step.

### Planet 3: Marcus Vane (54, Sovereign Fund Lead Investor) — *Gravitational Pull on Axis 2 (Monastic Mask vs. Tyrant)*
- **Function:** Julian's foil. Marcus makes no pretense of saving humanity; he wants a $40B IPO. Whenever Julian hides behind noble philosophical language, Marcus strips the mask off and forces Julian to admit they are in the exact same business.

### Planet 4: Lukas Meier / "Patient #14" (62, Retired Watchmaker) — *Embodiment of the Negation of the Negation (`--`)*
- **Function:** Physically rejuvenated to the biological vitality of a 35-year-old (`+`), yet stripped of every emotional bond, craft memory, and capacity for love (`--`). He is the living ghost of Julian's Controlling Idea: **immortality that feels worse than death.**
